About AgriConnect

AgriConnect is an agribusiness start-up that connects smallholder farmers directly with buyers: households, hotels, schools and exporters.

Farmers list their produce and set their own prices. Buyers browse, search, order and receive a receipt. We approve every farmer and listing so buyers can trust what they see.

Our promise

  • Fair prices for farmers
  • Fresh produce for buyers
  • Simple, mobile-friendly ordering
